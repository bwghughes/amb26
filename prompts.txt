MAC INITIATIVE BUILDER — WHAT TO SAY
Three sentences build the app. Say them out loud, or paste them.

First, in Safari: open the contest site, enter your team name and the PIN
on the projector. Keep that tab. The steps live there.


================================================================================
FIRST, ONCE — WAKE UP THE ASSISTANT
================================================================================

Read AGENTS.md, Ambassadors26/BUILD-SPEC.md, and SEED.md. Follow them whenever I
ask for something, and prefer the exact APIs they name. Reply with just “ready”.


================================================================================
PROMPT 1 — KEY POINTS
================================================================================

There are notes from a sales call in the left-hand pane. Make the Build initiative
button actually read them and give me proper fields I could drop straight into our CRM,
with how urgent this customer is right at the top — and all of it has to happen on
this Mac, nothing leaves the laptop.

  Then: Use sample → Build initiative → TURN THE WI-FI OFF → do it again.


================================================================================
PROMPT 2 — FOLLOW-UP EMAIL
================================================================================

Good. Now write the follow-up email I’d actually send this customer after that
conversation, and let me copy it or open it in Mail.

  Then: Copy, and paste it somewhere. Open in Mail.


================================================================================
PROMPT 3 — RECORD THE CALL
================================================================================

I don’t want to type my notes. Let me just talk to it and have it write them down
while I’m speaking — and again, all on this Mac.

  Then: Record call → allow the microphone → talk in this session's world
  (the line is in SEED.md; Use sample shows the customer). Hospital fallback:

They’ve got about three hundred Windows laptops, most of them four years old.
The help desk is drowning. And they’re nervous about patient data leaving the building.

  Words should appear as you speak. Stop recording → Build initiative.

  Microphone fails? Use sample, then skip to Make it yours / Submit.


================================================================================
PROMPT 4 — MAKE IT YOURS
================================================================================

This has to stand out on a wall of screenshots. Keep every button working, but
give the window a look that is obviously ours — start from SEED.md and go
further if you like.

  Behind? Skip this. The seed already changed the title, colour, and customer.


================================================================================
SUBMIT
================================================================================

Turn Wi-Fi back on. Stay on the contest tab. Three panes filled, and it
shouldn't look like default grey. ⌘⇧4 then Space, click the app window.
Attach the PNG on the Submit step. You should see it on the wall.


================================================================================
IF IT GOES WRONG — SAY ONE OF THESE
================================================================================

It built something odd ............ "Have another look at BUILD-SPEC.md — that isn't
                                     what it asks for."
Build failed ...................... paste the error, then: "fix this"
It asked for an API key ........... "No. This has to run on the Mac, on device.
                                     No network calls."
It broke something that worked .... "You've broken something that was working —
                                     put it back."
Two failed attempts ............... git checkout .    then say it again
Speech only after you stop ........ "Use SpeechAnalyzer, not SFSpeechRecognizer.
                                     I want the words to appear while I'm still talking."


================================================================================
TALKING TO IT INSTEAD OF TYPING
================================================================================

Press the dictation key (or Edit > Start Dictation), say the sentence, press it again.
The dictation is on-device too — which is the same thing you're selling.
