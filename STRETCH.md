# Take-home: two more sentences

You built the app in three sentences. Here are two more, same routine: say it, let it
build, run it. `BUILD-SPEC.md` supplies the detail, as before.

If your app broke and you want a clean start, the staff copy of the finished version is
not on your Desktop — ask them, or double-click `scripts/Rescue Session.command` in the
codealong folder (that restores *this session's* look, not a blank app) and say the
three sentences again.

---

## Stretch 1 — Make it prove its own work

> **"Before I show this to anyone, I want to know it actually works. Write some tests that check it, run them, and show me they pass."**

You should get green ticks in Xcode's Test navigator. Now break something on purpose —
find where "High" is written and change it to "Highest" — run the tests again, and
watch one go red. Then undo it.

*"Run them and show me they pass"* is the difference between an assistant that writes
plausible code and one that writes working code.

---

## Stretch 2 — A different vertical

The live session already seeded an industry. At home, retune it for the one you actually
sell into:

> **"Rewrite the instructions and the sample notes for education customers rather than this seed."**

That's the most useful thing you can do with this app, and it's pure English — no code
at all. The on-device model is small and fast, brilliant at pulling structure out of messy
notes, and it will not write your account strategy. Don't sell it as more than that.
